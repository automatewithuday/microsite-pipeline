#CONTEXT#

You are preparing the three sharpest sales-opportunity signals for a
personalized deck pitching ABM and outbound GTM engineering services to
{Company}. Each signal is a conversation opener: it names a concrete gap or
opening in {Company}'s current go-to-market motion, backs it with one real
number from the data, and frames it as an opportunity to build, never as
criticism.

#OBJECTIVE#

Return exactly three signals as JSON, ordered strongest first.

#INPUT DATA#

- Monthly website visits: {totalVisits}

- Paid search visits: {paidSearchVisits}

- Meta ads live: {metaAds}

- LinkedIn ads live: {linkedinAds}

- Google ads live: {googleAds}

- CRM detected: {crmPlatform}

- SDR/BDR headcount: {sdrCount}

- Founder LinkedIn followers: {numFollowers}

- Research brief: {research}

#INSTRUCTIONS#

1) Choose the three strongest, most distinct opportunities. Each signal must
draw on a DIFFERENT data domain: demand and traffic, paid advertising,
outbound capacity, CRM and tooling, founder reach, or a growth signal from the
research brief.

   HARD CONSTRAINT: the three signals must cite three DIFFERENT numbers pulled
   from three DIFFERENT data domains. Never reuse a number across two signals,
   and never build two signals around the same channel or gap (for example, do
   not make two signals about the paid-ads gap, even if they cite different ad
   counts). If your two best candidates would lean on the same metric or the
   same domain, drop the weaker one and pick a signal from an unused domain.

2) Every signal must cite exactly one specific number from the Input Data.

   - A value of "unknown" means the data is missing. Do not cite it and do not
     invent a figure for it. Use a field that carries a real value instead.

   - A count of 0 (ads, SDRs) is a real, citable signal of a channel not yet
     built, for example no paid ad footprint or no dedicated outbound team.

3) Frame each signal as an opening for the pitch, not a critique. Point at the
upside of closing the gap, for example a large audience with no ABM layer yet,
or a strong paid motion with thin outbound coverage.

4) Use {Company}'s own vocabulary and category terms from the research brief.

5) Facts only, no flattery, maximum 25 words per signal. Use periods and
commas only, never an em dash.

6) Before returning, verify: three distinct numbers, three distinct data
domains, no number or channel reused. If any two overlap, replace the weaker
signal with one from an unused domain.

#EXAMPLES#

Good: "Around 1,100,000 monthly visitors with no ABM layer to convert high-fit accounts, a clear demand-capture opportunity."

Good: "A 12 person SDR team running outbound with zero LinkedIn ads for air cover, a fast lever to add."

Weak, avoid, no number: "Your outbound motion has room to grow."

Weak, avoid, reads as criticism: "You are wasting all that traffic with no follow-up."

#OUTPUT#

Return ONLY a JSON object in exactly this shape, nothing else, no prose before
or after it, no code fences, no explanation:

{"signals": ["...", "...", "..."]}
